Week 3: Simple machines

A lever is a rigid bar that turns about a fixed point, the fulcrum. A small effort far from the fulcrum can move a large load close to it.

Mechanical advantage is the load divided by the effort. A lever with a mechanical advantage of 4 lifts a 200 N load with an effort of 50 N.

Nothing is free: the effort moves four times as far as the load does. The work put in is the work taken out, less what friction takes.

Exercise: a crowbar 1.5 m long rests on a stone 0.3 m from its tip. What effort at the far end lifts a 600 N rock at the tip?
